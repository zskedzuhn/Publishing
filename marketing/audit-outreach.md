# Catalogue Audit — Outreach Kit (August 2026)

Goal: 2 audit sales = $994. Send 10 personalized pitches to artists/managers you know.
Rule of thumb: mention something specific about *their* catalogue in the first line —
generic blasts get ignored, specifics get replies.

Best-fit targets: artists with 15+ releases, anyone who switched distributors or
changed artist names, artists with lots of collabs/features, and managers with rosters.

## Content angles (TikTok/Reels/Shorts — drive to blog + audit page)

1. **"The MLC is holding hundreds of millions in unmatched royalties. Here's why some
   of it might be yours."** → walk through the 3 registration systems in 45 sec →
   "full checklist on the blog, link in bio."
2. **"Your distributor did NOT register your song."** The #1 misconception. Distribution
   ≠ PRO ≠ MLC ≠ SoundExchange — four separate things, show each on screen.
3. **"One typo in your co-writer's name can freeze a song's royalties."** Show two
   registrations disagreeing → conflict → payment held. CTA: split sheet on every song.
4. **"You got sampled / synced and have no split sheet. What now?"** → the $7 template.
5. Post the checklist article to r/WeAreTheMusicMakers / r/musicmarketing as pure value
   (no link spam — share the steps, mention the blog only if asked or in a comment).
