Minimum Size
Never scale the full mark (glass + wordmark) smaller than 0.75 in / 20 mm wide in print, or 120px wide on screen. Below this the linework and lettering lose legibility.
Clear Space
Keep open space around the mark equal to the height of the letter "S" in "STEMS" on every side. No text, imagery, or edge of page may enter this zone.
Placement & Color
Use the black mark on Bone White or light backgrounds. Use an all-white reversed version on Ink Black or Dusk Blue backgrounds only. Never place on busy photography without a solid contrast panel behind it.

Staying On-Brand
Do
  • Use the logo only in its approved black or reversed-white versions, at or above minimum size, with full clear space respected.
  • Keep photography candid and low-light — golden hour, string lights, candlelit tables — over polished studio shots.
  • Pair Special Elite flyer headlines with Inter body copy, and let event flyers stay strictly black & white with a halftone/dot-screen texture.
Don't
  • Don't stretch, skew, rotate, recolor, or add drop shadows / outlines / gradients to the logo.
  • Don't place the mark on clashing colors, low-contrast backgrounds, or busy photography without a solid backing panel.
  • Don't substitute brand fonts with system defaults (Arial, Times New Roman) in finished materials, or mix in more than one accent color at a time.
